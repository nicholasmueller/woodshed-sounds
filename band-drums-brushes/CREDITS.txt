Band drums (brushes) - a Woodshed sound pack
==============================================

Brushes for ballads: Karoryfer Swirly Drums (kick, hi-hat, ride) with Frankensnare's brushed 14x5 maple snare (sweeps and taps).

Credit: Swirly Drums and Frankensnare by Karoryfer Samples, CC0 1.0
Licence: CC0 1.0
Made from: sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swirly-drums v1.104; sfzinstruments/karoryfer.frankensnare (commit 9151c2d)

This folder is a derivative of the library named above, made by Woodshed's
crates/backing/tools/build-packs.sh: our own flattened SFZ programs (every opcode
resolved, only the regions the Woodshed backing band can play) over the library's own
samples, converted to 16-bit FLAC, the drum mic positions premixed with the band's mic mix. The upstream licence and readme files are
included unchanged next to this file.
